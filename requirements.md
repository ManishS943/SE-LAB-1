# Complete requirements table

Transcribed from the original requirements document without adding source requirement IDs.

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance criteria | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall allow an authenticated Hostel Resident to log a plumbing, electrical, or carpentry ticket using an assigned-room tag, issue severity, description, geo-location tag, and optional photo attachment. | High | Pass: A complete ticket receives a unique ID, Reported status, timestamp, and Warden queue entry. Fail: A ticket is accepted without a room tag, category, or severity. | Captures actionable, location-specific information for maintenance triage. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall validate that the room tag belongs to the authenticated resident before creating a ticket. | High | Pass: A resident submits only for their assigned room; the room and geo-location are stored. Fail: A resident submits for another resident’s room. | Prevents false reports and gives staff the exact service location. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall allow a resident to view the status and chronological history of their own tickets, including Reported, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, and Closed. | Medium | Pass: The resident sees every saved status change with its timestamp. Fail: The history omits an update or exposes another resident’s ticket. | Keeps residents informed and reduces repeated enquiries. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall allow the Maintenance Warden to view, filter, prioritize, assign maintenance staff to, and update open tickets. | High | Pass: The Warden filters the queue, assigns staff, and saves an update visible in ticket history. Fail: The assignment or update is not retained. | Supports accountable dispatch and operational tracking. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall request resident closure sign-off when the Warden marks a ticket Resolved and shall close it only after confirmation or a documented Warden override. | Medium | Pass: Resident confirmation records a sign-off timestamp and changes the ticket to Closed. Fail: A ticket closes without sign-off or override reason. | Confirms repair quality and records an auditable closure decision. |
| NFR-001 | Performance & Reliability | The system shall detect every high-priority ticket with no status change for 24 hours and queue an escalation SMS/email to the Maintenance Warden within 5 minutes of the SLA breach. | High | Pass: With 100 overdue high-priority tickets under simulated peak load, every escalation is queued within 5 minutes and logged once. Fail: Any overdue ticket remains unalerted or receives a duplicate alert. | Ensures urgent issues cannot remain unattended. |
| NFR-002 | Security & Privacy | The system shall require authentication, enforce role-based access control, and restrict residents to viewing and changing only their own tickets and attachments. | High | Pass: A resident cannot access or alter another resident’s ticket by manipulating an ID or URL; Warden actions are audit logged. Fail: Cross-resident access or an unlogged Warden action succeeds. | Protects resident data and creates accountability. |

See `review_notes.md` for explicit clarifications and `traceability.md` for proposed acceptance scenarios. These scenarios have not been executed.
